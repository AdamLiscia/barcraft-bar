Built on the rail

Made by a bartender, for the middle of a shift.

I built BarCraft because I needed it at work. The biggest use case is a bartender needing a spec right now — everything in the app is built around that moment.

01

Instant spec lookup

Search a curated library of classics and modern classics in seconds. Search is the home screen, so the spec you need is one tap and a few letters away.

02

Make it yours

Tweak any classic to how you build it, or save your own specs and recipes — syrups, tinctures, infusions. Your version is yours; the original is always there to reset to.

03

Batch calculator

Scale any cocktail by drink count or by volume, with dilution handled. Load a spec, pick a size, and get the exact amounts — the same math I use for the batch program at work.

04

AI Mentor

A hospitality-minded assistant to talk through builds and balance — fix a drink that's too bitter, find a spirit-forward riff, or dial in a classic.
