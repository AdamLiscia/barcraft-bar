How it works

Four steps. You do one.

You tell me about the night. Everything after that — the menu, the shopping, the prep, the bar and the cleanup — is my job.

  1. Tell me about it

    Date, place, roughly how many people, and what kind of night it is. Two minutes on the form.

  2. We build the menu

    A short conversation about what your guests drink and what you want to spend. Three or four cocktails that work together.

  3. I handle the shopping

    I place your liquor order with your local store — exact bottles, sized to your headcount. You pay them; most deliver.

  4. I run the night

    Juice squeezed that day, cocktails batched ahead, the bar set, the night worked, and your kitchen left how I found it.

Who you're hiring

Trained in a room most people never see.

I'm Adam. I tend bar at Bedford Post Tavern, a Relais & Châteaux inn in Bedford, where I lead the batch cocktail program and make the house syrups. Twelve years in restaurants, eight of them behind a bar.

It's a program run with an order of operations for every drink, nothing free-poured, and every syrup weighed on a scale. That's where I learned exactly what a drink is supposed to taste like — which is why I can hit it fast at your party without holding up the line.
